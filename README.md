# Complex Product Random Matrices with Conjugate Mixing: From Quartic to Sextic Spectral Laws
arXiv

# Author
Takuya Kanazawa  

# Abstract
We study the complex random matrix ensemble $Q = \frac{1}{\sqrt{d}}\bigl(c_1XW+c_2\overline{XW}\bigr)+Z$, where $c_1,c_2\in\mathbb{C}$ are constants and $X\in\mathbb{C}^{n\times d}$, $W\in\mathbb{C}^{d\times p}$ and $Z\in\mathbb{C}^{n\times p}$ are mutually independent random matrices, each with \mbox{i.i.d.} complex Gaussian entries. The entries may have nonzero pseudo-variances, encoding the second-order couplings between the matrices and their complex conjugates. When $c_2=0$ and all matrices are real, this ensemble reduces to the well-studied product-plus-noise model, which arises in the analysis of shallow neural networks. Our ensemble is a widely linear extension of this single-channel model, incorporating the conjugate channel $\overline{XW}$. Under a standard self-averaging assumption for the resolvents, we perform a leave-one-out analysis to derive self-consistent equations for the asymptotic Stieltjes transform of $Q^*Q/n$ in the proportional limit $n,d,p\to\infty$. Their scalar reduction yields a sextic equation, in contrast to the quartic equation governing the single-channel model. We clarify how this equation reduces to lower-degree equations and recover the Marchenko--Pastur, product-Wishart and Pennington--Worah distributions as special cases. Numerical experiments confirm the theoretical predictions.
